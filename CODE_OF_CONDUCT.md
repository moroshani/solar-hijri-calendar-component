# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participation should be respectful, patient, and focused on improving the software. Welcome people regardless of experience, language, nationality, identity, or background.

Constructive participation includes:

- discussing technical disagreements without personal attacks;
- giving reproducible evidence and accepting corrections;
- respecting privacy and security boundaries;
- helping new contributors understand project conventions;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and unwanted disclosure of personal information.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other public spaces used for this project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `moroshaniofficial@gmail.com`. Include links or screenshots where appropriate. Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer, who may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.
